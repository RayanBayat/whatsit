Whatsit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-21

Whatsit explains text you select on web pages. Here is everything it does with data, in full.

What Whatsit processes

When you select text and choose What's this?, the extension reads:

  • the text you selected,
  • up to ~600 characters of surrounding text from the same paragraph,
  • the page title.

This is sent to Gemini Nano, Google's AI model running inside your browser on your own device, via Chrome's built-in Prompt API. The text is processed locally and is never sent over the network by Whatsit.

What Whatsit collects, stores, or transmits

Nothing.

  • No data ever leaves your device. The extension makes zero network requests.
  • Nothing is stored — no history, no logs, no cookies, no local storage beyond your own settings.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party services.
  • The extension only accesses a page when you explicitly invoke it there (activeTab); it has no standing access to any website.

The one-time, first-use model download is performed by Chrome, not by Whatsit, and contains no information about you or what you read.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly disclosed in the extension's listing.
